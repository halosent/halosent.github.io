# Live Orthodox, Server Rules

Post this in `#welcome` or `#debate-rules`. A short pin version is at the bottom.

## 1. General Conduct

**Be respectful, even in disagreement.** Attack the argument, not the person.

**No hate speech, slurs, or harassment.** Applies to anyone, no exceptions. Zero tolerance, no warning required.

**No doxxing.** Don't post anyone's real name, location, or other identifying details without consent.

**Keep it legal**, and within Discord's own Terms of Service.

**No spam or unsolicited self-promotion.** Occasional sharing is fine. Repeated advertising or invite spam gets you removed.

## 2. Debate Conduct

Debate channels carry a higher bar than general chat.

**Steelman before you respond.** Engage the strongest version of the other side's argument, not the weakest.

**Cite your sources.** Verses, translations, historical claims, and quotes should say where they are from.

**Stay on topic** within a thread, and take unrelated arguments to the right channel.

**No bad faith arguing.** Repeating a claim after it is been directly answered, without addressing the rebuttal, is not debate.

## 3. Roles

**Verified Debater** is opt-in for the debate channels, granted after agreeing to these rules. **Contributor** goes to site writers and trusted regulars, and is not automatic.

## 4. Moderation

Moderators can remove messages, mute, or restrict access in real time without lengthy explanation. Disagree with a call, take it to a DM, not the channel.

**Escalation:** warning, timeout, kick, ban. Hate speech, doxxing, and threats skip straight to a ban.
